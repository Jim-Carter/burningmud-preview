Connect

Burning runs on a telnet server. If you've never heard of telnet, that's fine: it's a plain-text connection, and any MUD client speaks it.

Hostburningmud.com
Port4000

At "Account name?" type new to create an account.

Pick a client

A client is the program you play in. Any of these will work. If you're unsure, take Mudlet.

Mudlet Free · Recommended

Modern client for Windows, Mac and Linux. The easiest start if you've never played a MUD.

mudlet.org

CMUD Paid · Windows

The modern replacement for zMUD. Strong scripting; many veterans use it.

zuggsoft.com

zMUD Paid · Windows

The classic. Still works, no longer developed.

zuggsoft.com

PuTTY Free · Windows

A plain telnet and SSH program. Connects fine, but has no triggers.

putty.org

TinyFugue Free · Linux

The long-time favourite on Burning. The Uzi trigger set is built for it.

sourceforge

TinTin++ Free · Linux, Windows

Command-line client with scripting; WinTin++ on Windows.

tintin.mudhalla.net

JuiceSSH Free · Android

Telnet and SSH client for Android. Add a telnet connection to burningmud.com, port 4000.

juicessh.com

Your computer's telnet Built in

Click Play now and your system opens its default telnet program. On Windows 10 and 11 you may need to switch the Telnet Client feature on first.

How to

Your first ten minutes

You start in the temple of Karandras. Type these in roughly this order.

  1. >look

    Read the room: its description, who's here and the obvious exits.

  2. >help

    Lists every help topic by category. help <word> explains any command.

  3. >look in chest

    The donation chest near the temple holds gear other players gave away. A better weapon makes the biggest difference early.

  4. >identify <item>

    Learn what an item does. Scrolls of identification are sold at the magic shop.

  5. >gossip hi, I'm new

    The public channel. Ask where to level, what to wear, where the shops are.

  6. >areas 1-10

    Zones suited to levels 1 to 10. The cartographer in each Temple City sells maps of the area.

  7. >recall

    Lost, or in the dark? Recall takes you back to your temple.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Burning runs under the Diku licence, which means it can't charge players, and it never has. Staff volunteer their time.

My character can't see. It's too dark.

Some races, like dwarves, have darkvision. Humans don't. Carry a light, learn the darkvision spell if your class has it, or type recall to get back to the temple.

Are there quests?

Questmasters around the world run dozens of automatic quests with unique rewards. Immortals also host live events: hide and seek, arena battles, and big fights against new threats.

Is there a way to win?

No. You set your own goals. Some players chase gear, some explore every area, some lead raids.

Will my character be deleted if I stop playing?

No. Characters and equipment are kept. Come back whenever you like.

Getting help

> who
[IMP] Bane         (an immortal is online)

Type who to see if an immortal is on, and send them a tell. No immortal online? Pray at the altar in any temple; your prayer is delivered when one logs in. Or email support@burningmud.com.
