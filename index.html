Burning is a free multiplayer game you play by reading and typing. Two continents, 400 areas, raids of fifty players, and a cavern twenty levels deep that changes shape every day. Online since 1 January 1996.

New to this? A MUD is an online world you read.

MUD stands for Multi-User Dungeon. MUDs came before graphical MMOs, and the good ones never stopped. Everyone plays in the same world at the same time, with no servers to pick and no instances to split you from your friends.

The first hour can feel strange, like the first chapter of a long book. Ask on gossip, read the in-game help, and it starts to feel natural fast.

  • CostFree. No shop, no subscription. Burning has never made a cent and never will.
  • DeviceAnything that can open a telnet connection: a PC, a Mac, a phone.
  • StyleFast, cooperative hack-and-slash. Roleplay is welcome but not required.
  • Your characterNever deleted. Leave for ten years and your gear will still be there.

<412hp 230m 160mv>enter well

The Well goes twenty levels down. It is different every day.

Under the surface lies a cavern of more than 6,000 generated rooms. Lava flows and cave-ins reshape it daily, and every level is harder and richer than the one above.

Creatures down there carry fragments of elemental essence. Collect enough and you can change your character's race.
